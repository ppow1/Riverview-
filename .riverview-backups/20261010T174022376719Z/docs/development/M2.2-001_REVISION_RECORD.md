## Intake and acceptance intent

The roadmap requires a verifier separate from the executor, evidence and acceptance criteria stored against the assignment, invalid output routed to rework, and completion only with verifier evidence. The worker currently leaves successful output in `awaiting_verification`; M2.1 never marks work complete.

## Design decision

Use a separate verifier capability and credential in the existing worker registry; reject verification by the executor identity. A deterministic verifier checks the M2.1 SHA-256 result envelope against the persisted attempt/input digest and recomputes the canonical result digest. Store the verifier identity/revision, attempt, decision, criteria, and evidence references in a dedicated versioned table. An accepted record is required before an assignment can enter `completed`; rejected results enter `rework_required`; inconclusive evidence remains blocked. Keep verifier behavior behind a replaceable protocol, with no external service or shared-schema migration.

## Acceptance criteria

- A valid independently recomputed result stores verifier evidence and transitions to `completed`.
- Tampered or invalid output is rejected and routes to `rework_required`.
- The executor cannot verify its own attempt; wrong scope/capability is rejected.
- Completion without an accepted persisted verification record is rejected in migrated M2.2 schemas.
- Verification is idempotent and records evidence durably.
- Concurrent unrelated assignments remain unaffected.
- Full suite, graph validation/synchronization, controlled revision audit, and push/PR CI pass.
