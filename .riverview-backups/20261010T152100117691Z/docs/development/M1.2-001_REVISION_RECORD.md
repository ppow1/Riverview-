## Roadmap requirements and acceptance criteria

Roadmap M1.2 requires versioned PostgreSQL migrations and durable assignment/event
storage aligned with the event contract, including authority reference, causality,
idempotency, and workflow metadata. Technical acceptance will require isolated
schema creation and migration; repeat/idempotent migration; rejection of checksum
drift; atomic state-plus-event transitions; rejection of illegal and duplicate
transitions; exact idempotency replay semantics; and persistence across reconnect.

The event contract is proposed, not a delegated authority service. This task may
persist authority references but must not claim to validate grants. It does not
implement M1.3 processor, M1.4 scheduling, worker execution, independent result
verification, dashboard, or notifications.
